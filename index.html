About the salon

A calm Montréal retreat for body and mind.

Salon de Massothérapie Ashley offers a quiet, clean and private environment where every visit is centered on comfort, attentive care and genuine relaxation. The atmosphere is intentionally understated—soft lighting, warm details and an unhurried rhythm that lets you disconnect from the day.

Whether your goal is to release everyday tension, enjoy more focused traditional bodywork, or share the immersive experience of a four-hands massage, the setting is designed to help you settle in and feel at ease.
